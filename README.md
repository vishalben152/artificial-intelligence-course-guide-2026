Understanding the Essence of Artificial Intelligence

Artificial Intelligence is the simulation of human intelligence in machines programmed to think like humans and mimic their actions. The idea of AI is not merely about machines performing tasks; it encompasses a range of technologies such as machine learning, natural language processing, robotics, and computer vision. These technologies enable systems to analyze data, make decisions, and improve their performance over time.
